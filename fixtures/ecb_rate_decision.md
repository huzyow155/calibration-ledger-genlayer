# European Central Bank Monetary Policy Decisions

## Interest Rate Decisions
The Governing Council today decided to lower the three key ECB interest rates by 25 basis points. Accordingly, the Governing Council decided to decrease the deposit facility rate by 25 basis points to 3.25 percent. The interest rates on the main refinancing operations and the marginal lending facility were lowered to 3.40 percent and 3.65 percent respectively.

The incoming information on inflation shows that the disinflationary process is well on track.

# Federal Reserve FOMC Monetary Statement

## Policy Action
Recent indicators suggest that economic activity has continued to expand at a solid pace. Job gains have moderated, and the unemployment rate has moved up but remains low.

In support of its goals, the Federal Open Market Committee decided to lower the target range for the federal funds rate by 25 basis points to 4.50 to 4.75 percent. The Committee will carefully assess incoming data, the evolving outlook, and the balance of risks.

# Starship Flight 6 Mission Report

## Mission Overview
SpaceX completed the sixth integrated flight test of Starship on the designated suborbital trajectory.

## Super Heavy Booster Operations
During the descent phase, automated flight criteria were not satisfied. In accordance with safety protocols, engineers confirmed the booster catch attempt was stood down due to safety sensor criteria. The Super Heavy booster executed a controlled divert maneuver and made a soft water landing in the Gulf of Mexico.

## Ship Upper Stage
The Starship upper stage successfully relit a single Raptor engine in space and concluded a controlled splashdown in the Indian Ocean.
